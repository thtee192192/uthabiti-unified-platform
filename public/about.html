About Us

Uthabiti Africa is an umbrella organization dedicated to driving structural transformation and operational resilience across the continent's Care Economy.

Our mission is to build the institutions, partnerships and digital infrastructure that strengthen Africa's Care Economy. We envisage an Africa where every woman working in care is recognised, protected, financially included and digitally connected, and where every child has access to quality care because the care economy itself is strong, trusted and sustainable.

Everything we build begins with one principle: Women and children belong at the centre of the Care Economy. Around them we build the systems that make quality care possible:

  • Organised caregiver networks
  • Professional development
  • Financial inclusion
  • Social protection
  • Digital services
  • Public policy
  • Trusted partnerships
  • Research and evidence

Together these create an integrated Care Economy that supports families, communities and economic growth.
